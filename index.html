About me

I make gameplay that feels smooth, fun and alive, and I lead the teams that ship it.

I started with VR architecture in 2017 and now lead Meta Quest games at Titanz Games. In between I built hyper-casual hits, 5v5 shooters and a metaverse with concerts for 1,000+ players. I care about clean, fast C#, multiplayer that holds up under load, and games that run well on low-end phones.

Game development isn't just my job. I truly enjoy it, and I'm always excited to bring new ideas to life.
